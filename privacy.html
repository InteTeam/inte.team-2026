Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to book, repair and return your device, and to answer your questions. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone. This website has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

2. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to anyone, for any product or purpose. There is no advertising network, data broker or analytics service on this website.

3. What we collect, and why

Booking, enquiry and call-back forms

When you book a repair, a visit or a call-back, we collect your name, email address and phone number, a description of your device or enquiry, the best time to call you, how you prefer to be contacted (phone, email or WhatsApp) and the appointment time you choose. We use this to arrange and carry out the job and to keep you updated.

Repair records

When we take in your device, we keep a record of the device, the fault, our diagnosis, the estimate you approve, the work done, payment and collection. We need this to do the job, honour any warranty and keep the business records the law requires.

The data on your device

We focus strictly on the fault. During a board repair, if the storage itself isn't damaged, we don't access your files. If a backup is needed before we start, we ask you first. We cannot confirm your data is intact until diagnosis is complete, so please back up your device before handing it in if you can.

Website chat

The chat button on our pages opens a chat window served by our own booking system. Questions you type are answered automatically, using only our own knowledge base, and those answers are marked as automatic. The answers are produced by AI models that run on our own hardware in Livingston. Your messages are never sent to an outside AI provider.

We are introducing an option to leave a message for our team in the chat. If you use it, you can choose to give your email address. We confirm it with a 6-digit code, then send you a copy of your message and our reply.

Phone, email, SMS and WhatsApp

When you contact us, we keep the conversation so we can answer you and refer back to it during your repair. We do not record phone calls. If we ever start recording calls or using automated voice answering, we will update this policy first and tell you at the start of each call.

Text messages are sent by our own SMS gateway in Livingston. Automatic messages from our booking system (for example booking confirmations and repair updates) use templates written by our team, filled in with your booking details. Our in-house AI may suggest a draft reply to our team, but a person always reads and edits it before anything is sent to you. AI never sends you a message by itself.

Car Boot Sale Livingston interest list

If you register interest in the community car boot sale, we collect your name, email address and selling/buying preferences, and use them only to plan the event and tell you about it. You can ask us to remove you at any time.

Visiting this website

Like any website, our servers and our network provider, Cloudflare, briefly record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use this to identify or profile you.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run the business, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions:

  • Hosting: our booking system, website and mail server run on servers rented from Contabo, located in Germany (EU).
  • Cloudflare: delivers and protects this website.
  • Google: stores encrypted backup copies of our booking database (Google Cloud Storage and Google Drive), and shows the maps on our pages if you choose to load them (Google Maps).
  • Email: sent from our own mail server (on the Contabo servers above). No outside email service handles your messages.
  • SMS: sent from our own gateway, using a SIM card on the Smarty mobile network, which delivers the messages.
  • Phone: our business phone line runs through Webex and Twilio. When we forward calls to a mobile, they are also carried by the Smarty mobile network.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to contact us by WhatsApp. We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, so your messages pass through Meta.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Dojo or SumUp; we never see or store your full card number. Bank transfers go to our Monzo Business account. You can also pay in cash.
  • Drop-off partners (Petzone, Craigshill and D&J Records, Bathgate): they receive your device and a booking reference only. They do not receive your name or contact details; everything else is handled by our booking system.
  • Our accountant and HMRC, where the law requires it.

Our servers are in Germany, which UK law recognises as giving adequate protection to personal data. If any other provider processes data outside the UK, it does so under safeguards approved under UK law.

7. Cookies and storage in your browser

This website sets no cookies of its own. If you switch off animations, your browser remembers that choice on your own device; it is never sent to us. The booking forms and the chat window do not set cookies.

The maps on our pages are provided by Google. They load only when you press "Show map", so nothing is requested from Google until you choose to. Once a map is shown, Google may set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data, where we don't have to keep it by law;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • give you your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent, where we rely on it.

Email support@inte.team. We reply within one month. It's free in almost all cases.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

9. Keeping your data safe

Access to customer records is limited to our team, who need it for their work. The servers that hold customer data can be administered only through our private encrypted network (VPN). Backups are encrypted before they leave our servers.

We may update this policy when our services change. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
