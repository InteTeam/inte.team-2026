Why We Do It.

Facts tell, but our stories sell the truth: We exist for our customers. There have been nights where we've spent 10 hours hunched over a single logic board, tracing a microscopic fault that others said was "unfixable." We only charged for three.

Why? Because we follow Intecracy—a philosophy that teaches us to give back to Livingston. Every device we save is a victory against e-waste and a win for the local family who doesn't have to spend £500 on a new machine.

We are proud to teach the next generation that "broken" is just a temporary state. With over 600 videos on YouTube, you can watch our process and see the integrity we put into every solder joint.

Turnaround Times

How long will my repair take?

Timescales depend on the priority you choose. Every job has two stages: diagnosis, then repair once you've approved the estimate.

1. Diagnosis

High

1–2 days. High-priority diagnosis is completed within 1–2 days of receiving your device.

Medium

3–5 working days, up to a week. This can take longer during busy periods.

Low

No fixed timescale. For customers with no deadline. Low-priority jobs are handled between scheduled work, so they can take considerably longer. You can upgrade to Medium at any time for an additional fee.

Diagnosis covers up to 1–2 hours of bench time. If that isn't enough to find the fault, we'll contact you to agree next steps before doing any further work.

2. Repair

High

1–2 days once diagnosed and approved. If parts need sourcing, fitting is completed within 1–2 days of them arriving.

Medium

3–5 working days, up to a week, from approval or from parts arriving. This can take longer during busy periods.

Low

No fixed timescale. Completed as capacity allows. Upgrade to Medium for an additional fee and we'll reserve dedicated bench time for your repair.

Parts delivery times are outside our control. We'll keep you updated if a part is delayed. High priority isn't available for every device type. See pricing for details.

⚡ Need it urgently?

Tell us. Urgent jobs go ahead of all other work, including evenings and weekends where needed. Urgent pricing is agreed with you before we start.
