Base rate is £40/h, dropping to as low as £30/h on jobs with a lot of hours. We build with LLM-assisted development within our frameworks rather than everything by hand — proven to run up to 500% faster than a senior developer working unassisted, which is what lets us pass the saving on to you.

Not sure which one you need?

A quick-fix is a single, well-defined change — a bug, a broken form, a small tweak — that we can usually scope over the phone in a few minutes. Anything bigger — a redesign, a new system, several moving parts — becomes a project quote instead. When you're not sure which side of the line your job falls on, just call us and we'll tell you honestly before any work, or billing, starts.

Turnaround, payment & hosting

  • Turnaround: typically within a week, though it depends on our availability that week — best to call or book to check, since some weeks we onboard several customers at once and others none.
  • Deposit: £100 to get started, collected after an initial conversation and a written email agreement — non-refundable.
  • Phased billing: we break the work into phases and you pay after each phase is confirmed complete, not one lump sum upfront. A staging environment is provided so you can test each phase before sign-off.
  • Hosting: we can host on our own infrastructure or on one of our rented UK/EU servers. Additional costs — hardware, software, third-party APIs, domain registration, Cloudflare DNS — are billed separately from the project price.
  • Already paying for something we can replace? If you're on a paid service we can move onto our own hosted equivalent (e.g. a paid cache service → self-hosted Redis, or a paid auth provider → Laravel's built-in authentication), we'll explain the difference, the benefit, and the cost in plain English before you decide.

Subscription & Rental

Our flagship model — a complete business web solution for a modest one-off setup fee, then a low monthly subscription instead of an expensive upfront build.

Established Business Package

£300 one-off setup + £30/month

Setup is £300 total, non-refundable, paid as three £100 instalments — each due once its phase is completed. Covers roughly 10 hours of work, sometimes more.

Included:

  • Landing page with a form
  • CRM that turns form submissions into bookings and emails confirmations to both your customers and you
  • Email server, email client and contacts, integrated across all your Inte.Team apps
  • Single sign-on (SSO) — one login across everything
  • Project management app
  • CMS — edit your landing page content yourself, via the same SSO login
  • Procurement app, if your business does purchasing
  • Warehouse logistics (WSL) built into the CRM — barcode and QR scanning and label printing
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) built in
  • Backups three times a day, one copy off-server — under 24 hours of potential data loss

More frequent backups, or a constant real-time second copy, are available for a custom fee. The £30/month covers infrequent small feature requests (a few a year) at no extra charge, and is a baseline tied to server usage — it only rises if your business grows and uses more resources. Once everything above is running perfectly, we'll work with you on your specific customer journey and build an extra app to simplify it, as a final phase — included if it's built from our existing ready-made solutions, quoted separately if it falls outside that.

The more apps — and AI agents — we build, the better prepared our customers are for a web that keeps changing, without needing to understand any of it themselves. Some have been with us 18 years. We also use the same software we sell, in our own business — including the systems that helped us grow our electronics repair side to three drop-off points, with more planned. We stand by what we build because we run on it too.
