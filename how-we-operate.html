    Booking

    You
    Book online or call us. Not in a rush? SMS, WhatsApp or email works too.
    We
    Send a confirmation email, usually within 5 minutes.
    Time
    About 2 minutes.

    Members contact us directly for priority care. If we have a spare, we'll swap your device for a working one while we repair yours. About membership →
  3. 2

    Drop-off

    Three places, depending on your visit.

    • Livingston HQ First visit. By appointment: we call to arrange a time. TV, Hi-Fi and High priority repairs come here only.
    • Petzone, Craigshill Shopping Mall Returning customers. Book online, drop off, show your booking ID. No call needed.
    • D&J Records, Bathgate Returning customers in the Bathgate area. Same as Petzone.

    Drop-off points only see your booking ID and when you'll arrive. They don't test devices and never take payment.

  4. 3

    Diagnosis

    We
    Test the device and trace the fault on the bench.
    Time
    High: 1–2 days. Medium: 3–5 working days, up to a week. Low: no fixed timescale. Turnaround times →
    Cost
    A diagnosis fee for your device type. It is non-returnable. Repair pricing →

    Diagnosis covers up to 1–2 hours of bench time. If that isn't enough to find the fault, we'll contact you to agree next steps before doing any further work.

  5. 4

    Estimate

    We
    Agree the estimate with you before we repair.
    You
    Nothing more, as long as the repair stays within the agreed estimate. If it would cost more, we contact you first.
    Cost
    Usually nothing up front. If a deposit or a fee is needed, we tell you first and email a payment link to your booking email. We carry on once it's paid.
  6. 5

    Repair

    We
    Fix it: parts, soldering, board-level work.
    Time
    High: 1–2 days once diagnosed and approved. Medium: 3–5 working days, up to a week, from approval or from parts arriving. Low: no fixed timescale.

    Parts delivery times are outside our control. We'll keep you updated if a part is delayed. Need it urgently? Ask us about Urgent.

  7. 6

    We
    Stress test the device and give it a final inspection before it goes back to you.

Good to know

Paying

Always with us, never at a drop-off point. Card, bank transfer or cash. When something has to be paid up front, we email a payment link to your booking email and carry on once it's paid.

Deposits

We try not to take deposits. When one is needed, mostly for TVs and lower-value items, we tell you before we start.

Diagnosis fee

The diagnosis fee is non-returnable, even if you decide not to go ahead with the repair.

30 days to collect

You have 30 days from our "ready for collection" message. If you can't collect in time, let us know. If we hear nothing within 30 days, the device is treated as donated to Inte.Team: any personal data on it is wiped, then it is recycled or disposed of.

Warranty

It depends on the repair: who supplied the parts, new or used, and whether it was a part swap or a board-level repair. We explain yours at collection.

Messages from us

We keep you updated by SMS. If SMS isn't possible, we email you instead. If our first email lands in Spam/Junk, move it to your inbox so the rest reach you.
