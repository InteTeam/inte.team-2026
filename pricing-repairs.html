Electronics Repair Pricing

These are real figures from what our customers have actually paid — not vague estimates. Every device is diagnosed first, and no work starts without your approval.

How priority pricing works

Most repairs come in two tiers: Medium (3–5 working days, up to a week, when parts don't need sourcing) and High (same/next day, or same-hours where possible — costs Medium price +50%). Industrial/commercial work also has an Urgent tier — negotiable, for when we drop everything and come to you. Location can affect price too: drop-off partner points sometimes run slightly higher than our Livingston HQ (noted where it applies).

Members: spare device while we repair yours

If you're a member and need a loaner device during your repair, we may have one in stock — mainly TVs, laptops, PS5, Xbox Series consoles, gamepads (PS5/Xbox + Nintendo Switch Joy-Cons), and Sony TA-FE series hi-fi amps (phones and tablets coming soon). Call us or book online to check availability and pricing — it isn't automatic.

Samsung TVs — please call first

Edge-lit Samsung TVs commonly develop a melted-diffuser problem, and panel quality on Samsung sets from roughly the last 5 years is inconsistent — some customers choose to donate rather than repair. We'll always talk it through with you by phone before booking a Samsung TV in.

Your data, handled carefully

If you haven't backed up your own data, we may need to back it up to an external source before we start — this can add to the cost. During a board repair, if the storage itself isn't damaged, we don't touch or access your data or files; we focus strictly on the fault. We can't confirm your data is intact until diagnosis is complete.

Working with us on B2B terms

  • 60-day invoice terms accepted in some cases, once an email agreement or a simple contract is signed.
  • Need us to sign an NDA? Just let us know up front. Our staff, contractors and apprentices already have NDAs signed with Inte.Team internally.
  • Fully mobile: microscopes, oscilloscopes, digital multimeters, hot air stations, professional soldering stations (Aifen A9-Pro/Plus with C210 and 245 tips), high-resolution thermal cameras, logic analyzers, programmers (RT809H with 50+ adapters, SVOD4), bench power supplies and portable fume extraction — all ready to load into the car.
  • Unleaded Polish solder (Cynel) most of the time; leaded solder used for medical, military, or older repairs, per British law.
  • Thousands of regular and SMD components in stock and growing, covering most typical general-electronics faults.
